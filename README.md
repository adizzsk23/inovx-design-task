## Deliverables

Every panel produces all three formats, on their own panel theme.

| Deliverable | Dimensions | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram Story | 1080 x 1920 px | Vertical. Keep key content clear of the top and bottom UI zones. |
| Instagram Post | 1080 x 1080 px (or 1080 x 1350 px) | Square or portrait, your call. |
| Idea Factory Display | 5760 x 1120 px | Ultra-wide LED strip. Recompose the layout for this ratio — do not stretch or scale a square design to fit. |

The three formats do **not** have to share a single visual identity. Tying them together into one system is welcome, but it is optional. What matters is that each poster stands on its own.

## Rules

- **No AI-generated work.** No AI images, AI layouts, or AI-generated type. Design it yourself.
- **Inspiration is fine, copying is not.** Looking at past INOVX posts to understand the tone is encouraged. Reproducing someone else's layout, type treatment, or artwork is not.
- **Submission is individual.** You are grouped into panels only to assign themes. Every person submits their own set of three formats.

## How to Upload

Submit **one PDF containing all three formats** — story, post, and display, in that order. Name it `Panel1_YourName.pdf`, using your own panel number and your own name. For example, a Panel 2 member named Kamakshi uploads `Panel2_Kamakshi.pdf`.

Two things to sort out before you start:

- **Keep the PDF under 25 MB.** That is GitHub's limit for a browser upload. If your export is larger, reduce the image quality on export rather than dropping any of the three formats.
- **Use a computer, not the GitHub phone app.** The mobile app cannot upload files. A phone browser can work if you switch it to desktop mode, but a laptop is far less painful.

Steps, in the browser:

1. Sign in to GitHub. If you do not have an account, create a free one at github.com first.
2. Open this repository page and click the `Design` folder.
3. Click your panel's folder — for example, `Panel-1-Crack-the-Case`.
4. Near the top right of the file list, click the **Add file** button, then choose **Upload files**.
5. Drag your PDF into the upload area, or click **choose your files** and pick it from your computer.
6. Check the file name shown on screen. It must read `Panel1_YourName.pdf` with your panel number and name.
7. Scroll to the bottom, to the **Commit changes** box. Leave the default message or type something like `Add Panel1_YourName.pdf`.
8. Click the green **Commit changes** button.
9. The page reloads and your PDF appears in the folder list. That means it is submitted.

If something goes wrong, repeat the steps and upload again rather than leaving a broken file in place.

## Judging Criteria

- **Typography and hierarchy** — type choices, spacing, and whether the eye lands on the right thing first.
- **Format sense** — each size composed for its own ratio, not resized from one master file.
- **Mood** — whether the design carries the feeling its theme calls for.
- **Finish** — alignment, margins, export quality, and general care in the details.
